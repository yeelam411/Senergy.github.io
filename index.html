What is SEN?

Students with Special Educational Needs (SEN) are those who may face difficulties in their studies or daily lives and require additional educational support. These difficulties can stem from a variety of causes, including physical or mental illnesses, disabilities, or impairments.

The number of students with Special Educational Needs (SEN) in Hong Kong has experienced a steep upward trajectory, far exceeding previous estimates. While there were approximately 60,000 students in the 2021/22 school year, recent data from the Education Bureau reveals that by the 2023/24 school year, the number of SEN students in public sector ordinary schools alone has surged.

Primary student numbers rose by 32.2% and secondary students by 37.9% compared to just five years prior, with current estimates for the 2025/26 cycle suggesting that over 10% of the total student population now requires specialized support.

Despite this growth, deep-seated negative stereotypes and stigmatizations persist, creating a perception gap that hinders effective intervention. For parents, these social pressures lead to a measurable lack of confidence; many fear that a formal diagnosis will label their child for life, limiting future academic and career opportunities.

Our Mission

SENERGY is dedicated to bridging the gap between SEN students, their parents, and the support they need. We aim to foster empathy through storytelling and provide a clear roadmap for families seeking professional assistance in Hong Kong. We want to centralize the resources of community centers so that parents and students can more easily learn about the activities offered by different community centers and make it easier for them to participate.
