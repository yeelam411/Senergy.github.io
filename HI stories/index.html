Introduction

Hearing Impairment occurs when there is a problem with one or more parts of the ear or the nerves that send sound signals to the brain.

Hearing loss is not a reflection of intelligence. Students with HI have the same range of intellectual ability as their peers; they simply access information through different channels—often relying more on visual cues.

Types of Hearing Loss:

  • Conductive: Sound is blocked from passing through the outer or middle ear, often due to fluid or wax.
  • Sensorineural: Damage to the sensitive hair cells in the inner ear or the auditory nerve. This is usually permanent.
  • Unilateral vs. Bilateral: Hearing loss in only one ear (unilateral) or both ears (bilateral).
  • Fluctuating: Hearing that changes from day to day (common with "glue ear").

Communication Styles:

  • Oral/Aural: Using speech and residual hearing (often with hearing aids).
  • Sign Language: Using a visual language like BSL or ASL.
  • Total Communication: A mix of signs, speech, lip-reading, and gestures.

How Parents Can Help:

  • Face-to-Face Contact: Always ensure your child can see your face before you start speaking. Avoid talking from another room.
  • Check Equipment: Make it a daily habit to check that hearing aids or cochlear implant batteries are working and the devices are clean.
  • Reduce Background Noise: Turn off the TV or noisy appliances when having a conversation to help your child focus on your voice.
  • Advocate for Subtitles: Ensure that any videos watched at home or school have captions enabled to provide a visual anchor for the audio.

A Note to Students!!!

Being deaf or hard of hearing is a different way of experiencing the world. While you might miss some sounds, you often develop an incredible "visual eye" for detail. Communication is a two-way street; it is not just about you hearing others, but about others learning how to reach you. From famous musicians like Beethoven to modern-day actors and activists, many people have shown that silence or partial sound does not stop you from having a loud and powerful voice in the world!
